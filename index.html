Physical Apple device + smart bead board Bluetooth pairing and workflow (Guideline 2.1).

中文说明

本演示中的硬件是一块智能拼豆板。它像一块显示屏，用于显示拼豆图纸，帮助用户按图完成拼豆。

用户可以在 App 中切换不同的图纸；智能拼豆板会同步显示对应内容，方便对照拼豆。

在「智能拼豆板」界面：

· 右上角的重新选择图纸按钮：可切换不同的拼豆图纸。

· 底部的色号条：可切换不同颜色；对应图纸中该颜色的豆子会高亮显示在智能拼豆板上，方便用户按色号依次拼豆。

English

The designated hardware in this demo is a smart bead board. It works like a display that shows bead patterns so users can follow along while placing beads.

Users can switch between different patterns in the app, and the smart bead board syncs the selected pattern to make assembly easier.

On the Smart Bead Board screen:

· The Reselect Pattern button at the top right switches to a different pattern.

· The color-code bar at the bottom switches colors. Beads of the selected color in the pattern are highlighted on the smart bead board, so users can place one color at a time.
